Overview

Beehiiv access manual

Use this guide when Beehiiv asks you to verify your email before reading, liking, or commenting on a published Altitude issue.

Before you start

Beehiiv checks every reader against your Skyfare subscription before unlocking likes and comments. Have the same email address tied to your Altitude membership ready, and keep that inbox open in case a verification code arrives.

Open Latest Issues
Step 01

Choose “Read on Beehiiv”

Open any Altitude issue and look above the article for two reading options. Select Read on Beehiiv instead of Read on Skyfare — this is what takes you to Beehiiv's site, where liking, commenting, and the community itself live.

Step 02

Let Beehiiv verify your subscription

As soon as you land on Beehiiv, it runs a quick verification check to confirm you're subscribed to Skyfare before unlocking the issue. This happens automatically — there's nothing to fill in yet.

Step 03

Skyfare confirms your email and tier

Once verification passes, Skyfare loads behind the scenes to check that your email is subscribed and confirm your membership tier before handing you back to the issue.

Note: Beehiiv may occasionally ask for a one-time passcode. If it does, check the inbox for the email you entered — Beehiiv sends a 6-digit OTP valid for a few minutes. Enter it to continue.

Step 04

Find the like and comment bar

Scroll to the bottom of the issue. That's where the like and comment controls live — Beehiiv's way of joining the conversation on that issue.

Step 05

Set up your community profile

Before your like or comment posts, Beehiiv asks for a few basics: your First and Last name, plus an optional avatar. Fill these in once and you're set for every future issue.

You're ready to go

Welcome to the Skyfare community — like, comment, and follow along on every Altitude issue from here on.

More questions?

If verification still isn't working the way it's described above, our team can help directly.
