Support and questions

Billing questions, access issues, Beehiiv verification, and account help for Altitude members.

Contact Support

Send a member support note. We usually reply within a few hours.

How do I update my payment method?
Open Manage Billing from the Membership page. It opens Stripe's secure billing portal, where you can update your card, view invoices, or change your payment method directly.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from the Stripe billing portal and you will not be charged again. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.
Why does Beehiiv ask me to verify again?
Skyfare verifies portal access separately from Beehiiv. Beehiiv may ask for the same email so you can use its native likes, comments, and reader session.
Can I access previous premium content?
Yes. Use the Latest Issues page to browse the member archive while your Altitude access is active.
