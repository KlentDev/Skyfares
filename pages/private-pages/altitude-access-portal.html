Billing questions, access issues, or anything else — we usually reply within a few hours.

How do I update my payment method?
Open Manage Billing from the Membership Plans section above — it opens Stripe's secure billing portal, where you can update your card, view invoices, or change your payment method directly.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — there's no lock-in contract. Cancel from the Manage Billing portal above and you won't be charged again; your access simply continues until the end of your current billing period.
What happens to my access after I cancel?
Nothing changes right away. You keep full Altitude access until your current period ends on the date shown in your membership summary above — after that, your account reverts to the free tier.
I bought the KrisFlyer Guide — why do I have Altitude access?
The KrisFlyer Guide includes 90 days of complimentary Altitude access as part of the purchase. It's a one-time bonus, not a subscription — see the Access Source panel in Membership Plans above for your exact expiration date.
I'm having trouble accessing my account.
Try refreshing this page first — occasionally billing details take a minute to sync after a payment. If the issue continues, contact support above and we'll sort it out.
