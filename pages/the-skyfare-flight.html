How it works

From story to boarding pass.

The process is simple. Share the journey that would mean something, and Skyfare reads every entry before choosing one person to fly.

  1. 01
    Application

    Tell us where you would go.

    Share the destination, the reason, and the part of the journey you would remember.

  2. 02
    Story review

    Your story is read by a person.

    The story matters more than polished writing. Honest and specific is enough.

  3. 03
    Selection

    One person is selected.

    One meaningful flight is chosen every three months from the current entries.

  4. 04
    Flight

    Someone gets to go.

    Skyfare helps arrange the flight experience for the selected traveler.

Write the part that matters

You do not need a perfect story.

A few honest details help us understand the journey you would choose and why it would stay with you.

01
Where would you go?

Name the place, route, or destination you keep thinking about.

02
Why this trip?

Tell us who you would take, what you would do, or what the journey would mean.

03
What should we know about you?

A few sentences are enough. Write as yourself, not like an application form.
