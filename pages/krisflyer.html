Your Options

Two ways to use KrisFlyer miles.

Nominee Redemption is usually best for confirmed ticketing. Direct Transfer is better when flexibility matters more than speed.

Recommended for speed

Nominee Redemption

Best when you want help securing a confirmed premium cabin ticket through an established nominee redemption path.

  • Skyfare manages the redemption flow
  • Useful for live route opportunities

Direct Transfer
