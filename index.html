    Common Questions

    Straight answers about nominee redemption, timing, and fees, before you reach out.

    Is nominee redemption legal?
    Yes. KrisFlyer's programme rules allow members to redeem miles for nominated individuals. We operate fully within the programme's terms, handling the search, strategy, and booking on your behalf.
    What if there's no award space on my dates?
    We propose 1-2 alternates (date or route) and use nominee redemption paths that avoid waitlists. Our experts find hidden availability that doesn't appear on standard search engines.
    How fast can I fly?
    Nominee bookings can be immediate once space is confirmed. Direct transfers typically complete in approximately 5 business days. For urgent travel, contact us via WhatsApp straightaway.
    How do your fees work?
    Travel Strategy Calls (S$99) determine feasibility. Itinerary Design (from S$240) handles deep strategy. Nominee redemptions follow a clear tiered rate. No hidden mark-ups.
