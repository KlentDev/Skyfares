    Good to Know

    Questions before you start.

    Clear answers about airline rules, availability, timing, and costs.

    Is nominee redemption legal?
    KrisFlyer permits members to redeem miles for nominated individuals under its programme rules. Skyfare confirms the applicable airline-compliant path before any booking moves forward.
    What if there's no award space on my dates?
    We look at nearby dates, alternate gateways, and other eligible options. The focus stays on bookable award space, with the trade-offs explained before you decide.
    What happens after I send my trip?
    Skyfare reviews the route, dates, cabin, and mileage options, then shares the most practical paths. Timing depends on award space and any transfer or nominee steps required.
    How do your fees work?
    The applicable Skyfare service fee is confirmed before work begins. Airline taxes, programme fees, and mileage requirements are shown separately when relevant. No hidden mark-ups.
