How the system works, in plain English

Think of it like an airport. The website is the terminal — where people arrive and decide where they're going. Cloudflare Workers is the control tower — it checks every request, routes it to the right place, and makes sure nothing crosses paths that shouldn't.

Stripe is the only system allowed to confirm a payment actually went through. Everyone else — Airtable, the website, the team — just reads that confirmation after the fact. Airtable is the passenger manifest: proof of who bought what, used by the team to run the business day to day.

Beehiiv handles the newsletter on its own track, separate from paying-customer records, because most subscribers never buy anything. And KV, R2, and Cloudinary are the baggage system — quietly holding access rights, guide PDFs, and images so nothing gets lost in transit.
